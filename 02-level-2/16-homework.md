# Level 2: Homework

## Why might a dog not "obey a command"? Give 10 possible reasons.

I've heard a lot of answers to this question in my lifetime. Most of them were along these lines:

- "She's lazy"
- "She's bored"
- "She's stupid"
- "She's stubborn"
- "She's too dominant"
- "She's blowing me off"
- "She's giving me the paw"

Before you start this homework, try to look at the question from the point of view of your dog – a dog who's interested in doing what you want her to do because doing what you want generally gets her what SHE wants. If your dog is In The Game, the 7 answers above are just blaming the victim and giving the handler an excuse for beating up the dog, either mentally, physically, or both. Can you think of better answers – answers that reflect the teamwork and trust you're building? If you're having trouble, begin by thinking of 10 reasons why YOU might fail to get a job done on a particular day.
