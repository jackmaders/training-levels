# Free-Shaping & Low-Impact Trick Lab — Steps to Success

_A force-free, step-by-step Side Quest building on Main Quest shaping theory and Tricks to grow your dog's creativity, fine-motor control, and problem-solving confidence._

## How This Side Quest Connects to the Main Quest

In the Main Quest, [The Thinking of Training](../../markdown/00-theory/07-the-thinking-of-training.md) introduces **shaping** (rewarding tiny approximations toward a goal without luring), and [Level 2 Tricks](../../markdown/level-2/tricks.md) asks you to plan and teach one fun trick.

This Side Quest turns shaping into a structured skill tree—keeping everything **low-impact, indoor-friendly, and brain-heavy**:

1. **Pure Free-Shaping Mindset:** Teaching your dog _how to learn_—that offering thoughtful interactions with props earns clicks, with zero luring, nagging, or physical prompting.
2. **Fine-Motor & Stationary Precision:** Crafting clean, adorable head, paw, and mouth tricks that exercise focus and body awareness without jumping or high-speed impact.
3. **Everyday Helper Chains:** Using back-chaining to turn individual behaviors into multi-step household routines (shutting drawers, pressing buttons, tidying toys).

## The Five Golden Rules of the Shaping Lab

1. **Stay Silent & Still:** During shaping, your body and voice go quiet. Your clicker does 100% of the talking.
2. **High Rate of Reinforcement:** Aim for 8–12 clicks per minute when starting a new behavior. If 15 seconds pass without a click, your criteria is too high—split smaller!
3. **Reset Deliberately:** Toss or place the treat slightly away from the prop (or drop it behind your dog's paws) so she gets a clean start for the next repetition.
4. **Never Lure Out of Impatience:** Switching from shaping to luring mid-session teaches the dog to stop thinking and wait for you to wave food in her face.
5. **Short, Joyful Sessions:** Two to three minutes of intense brainwork is plenty. End on an easy win before fatigue or frustration sets in.

## Curriculum

- [Level 1: The Creativity Game (101 Things to Do with a Prop)](level-1-the-creativity-game.md) — Orienting to props, shaping 3 distinct interactions on one object, handling extinction bursts cleanly, switching props, and naming a favorite behavior.
- [Level 2: Stationary, Head & Precision Tricks](level-2-head-mouth-and-stationary-tricks.md) — Cross paws, _"Say Your Prayers / Shy"_, blowing bubbles, chin-down object holds, and micro-expression captures.
- [Level 3: Everyday Helper Chains](level-3-everyday-helper-chains.md) — Pushing doors/drawers shut, pressing tap-lights or buttons, pulling a rope/towel, tidying toys into a basket, and building 3-part chains.
