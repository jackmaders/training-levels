# Everyday Citizenship & Play Levels — Steps to Success

_A force-free Side Quest combining constructive toy play with practical real-world manners (doorways, greetings, vehicle control, and roadcraft) to bridge the Main Quest with the UK Kennel Club Good Citizen Dog Scheme (Bronze, Silver, and Gold)._

## How This Side Quest Connects to the Main Quest

The Main Quest ([Training Levels](../../markdown/index.md)) builds deep self-control, loose-leash teamwork, and reliable stays. This Side Quest packages the **real-world civic and play protocols** tested in the Kennel Club Good Citizen Dog Scheme:

1. **Structured Toy Play & Arousal Switching:** Turning tug and toy engagement into a clean, mutually joyful reward system where your dog can gear up enthusiastically and settle calmly on cue.
2. **Thresholds & Equipment Etiquette:** Polite collar/lead attachment and formal doorway/gate holds before stepping into busy spaces.
3. **Controlled Greetings & Vehicle Control:** Greeting strangers and other dogs calmly without jumping, plus waiting politely at open car doors and boots.
4. **Roadcraft & Public Etiquette:** Automatic kerb stops, safe pavement positioning away from traffic, crossing roads calmly, and settling in public spaces.

## GCDS Responsibility & Equipment Checklist

Alongside the practical exercises in Levels 1–3, the Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers verify responsible dog ownership habits:

- [ ] **Identification Tag:** Flat collar fitted with a clear ID tag showing the owner's name, address, and postcode.
- [ ] **Microchip:** Registered with up-to-date contact details on an approved database.
- [ ] **Clean-Up Gear:** Waste bags carried on every walk.
- [ ] **Suitable Lead & Collar/Harness:** Well-fitted everyday walking gear (no harsh check chains or shock collars).
- [ ] **Care & Law Knowledge:** Understanding your dog's five welfare needs, local fouling/control laws, roadside safety, and polite public etiquette.

## Curriculum

- [Level 1: Structured Play & Doorway Thresholds](level-1-structured-play-and-thresholds.md) — Constructive toy engagement, clean toy drops, the play-to-settle switch, gate/doorway waits, and calm collar/lead handling.
- [Level 2: Controlled Greetings & Vehicle Control](level-2-greetings-and-vehicle-etiquette.md) — Greeting strangers politely, greeting when another dog is present, calm vehicle entry, and waiting at an open car door/boot.
- [Level 3: Road Walk, Kerbs & Public Etiquette](level-3-road-walk-and-public-etiquette.md) — Automatic kerb stops, crossing roads under control, walking on the inside of the pavement, roadside traffic proofing, and public café/bench settles.
