# Off-Lead Control & Heelwork Levels — Steps to Success

_A force-free Side Quest covering precision heelwork, the Emergency Stop, Positions on the Move, and the Hurdle Retrieve—skills intentionally left out of the Main Quest to bridge toward Kennel Club Gold, Club Platinum, and obedience sports._

## How This Side Quest Connects to the Main Quest

In [Level 2 Lazy Leash](../../markdown/level-2/lazy-leash.md), Sue Ailsby explicitly notes that formal **Heel** is a precision sport behavior rather than an everyday walking habit—so the Main Quest teaches a relaxed loose leash instead. Likewise, the Main Quest builds stationary distance sits/downs, jumps, and retrieves as separate behaviors.

This Side Quest adds the **high-precision off-lead toolkit** tested in the **Kennel Club Gold Award** and post-Gold **Club Platinum** modules:

1. **Close Heelwork & Walking Free:** Knowing how to find the exact "pocket" beside your leg, return cleanly to your side from a distance, and walk off-lead through turns, halts, and pace changes.
2. **The Emergency Stop (`Stop the Dog`):** Interrupting active running—both toward you and away from you—with an instant, reliable drop or stop at a distance.
3. **Platinum Precision & Hurdle Retrieve:** Sustaining a 30-second focused **Watch** around dog distractions, dropping into **Sit**, **Down**, or **Stand** _while walking_, and combining jumping with formal retrieving.

> [!TIP]
> **Keep Lazy Leash and Heel Distinct!**
> Use a clear verbal cue (**Heel** or **Close**) and specific start/end cues (such as **OK** or **Free** to release) so your dog never confuses short, focused **Heelwork** drills with relaxed **Lazy Leash** sniffing walks.

## Curriculum

- [Level 1: Heel Position & Walking Free](level-1-heel-position-and-walking-free.md) — Pivoting into heel position, returning to handler's side, 1–5 step heelwork, turns/pace changes, and off-lead walking free.
- [Level 2: Emergency Stop (Stop the Dog)](level-2-emergency-stop-at-distance.md) — Back-step drops, interrupting a recall, variable reward (80/20 rule), stopping while moving _away_, and outdoor 20-foot stops.
- [Level 3: Platinum Positions on the Move & Hurdle Retrieve](level-3-platinum-positions-and-hurdle-retrieve.md) — Sustained 30-second **Watch**, Sit/Down/Stand on the move, 15-foot distance position box, and retrieve over a jump.
