# Off-Lead Control Level 3: Platinum Positions on the Move & Hurdle Retrieve

## Comebefores

Complete [Off-Lead Control Levels 1](./level-1-heel-position-and-walking-free.md) and [2](./level-2-emergency-stop-at-distance.md), plus [Level 3 Jump](../../markdown/level-3/jump.md), [Level 3 Retrieve](../../markdown/level-3/retrieve.md), and [Level 4 Focus](../../markdown/level-4/focus.md) in the Main Quest.

## Where We're Going

- Step 1: Dog holds a sustained 30-second focused **Watch** around dog distractions.
- Step 2: Dog executes **Sit** and **Down on the Move** while you keep walking forward.
- Step 3: Dog executes **Stand on the Move** while you keep walking forward.
- Step 4: Dog performs a 3-position distance box (**Sit $\leftrightarrow$ Down $\leftrightarrow$ Stand**) at 15 feet.
- Step 5: Dog completes a formal **Retrieve Over a Jump** cleanly to hand.

## Think About Platinum Precision

While the official Kennel Club scheme finishes at Gold, many training clubs run an unofficial **Platinum** tier bridging into competitive obedience and working trials. These exercises require **body awareness, impulse control, and clean mechanics**:

- In **Positions on the Move**, the hardest part isn't for the dog—it's for the _handler_! Most handlers accidentally slow down, turn their shoulders, or stare backward when cueing a sit/down/stand on the move, which prompts the dog to follow instead of freezing in place.
- In the **Hurdle Retrieve**, jumping _out_ and jumping _back_ while holding an object cleanly tests self-control before the send, accuracy on the pickup, and polite delivery to hand.

## About the Cues

Keep cues crisp and distinct: **Watch** (sustained eye contact), **Sit**, **Down**, **Stand**, and **Get It** (paired with **Jump/Over** if needed at first).

## Steps

### Step 1: Sustained 30-second focused "Watch" under distraction.

Expand [Level 4 Focus](../../markdown/level-4/focus.md) for club-level focus drills:

1. Sit or stand beside your dog (in heel position or facing you) and cue **Watch**.
2. Build smooth eye contact to 10, 20, and 30 full seconds without breaking gaze—clicking and rewarding calmly so she stays relaxed rather than tense.
3. Add distractions in stages: another person walking past 10 feet away, someone tossing a soft toy nearby, and finally another calm dog sitting or working 10–15 feet away.

---

### Step 2: Sit and Down on the move (handler keeps walking).

Teach your dog to park instantly in **Sit** or **Down** while your legs keep marching smoothly forward:

1. **Start at 1 Step:** Walk 3 steps together in **Heel**, cue **Sit** (or **Down**), take **1 normal step forward** without turning your head, pause briefly, **Click** while she stays parked, and step _backward_ to feed her right where she sat/downed!
2. **Build Distance:** Gradually take 2 steps, 5 steps, and 10 steps smoothly forward after giving the cue before halting, turning to face her, and returning to her side to reward.
3. **Avoid Telegraphing:** Practice keeping your pace completely steady—do not slow down or lean backward right before saying **Sit** or **Down**.

> [!TIP]
> **Always Return to Reward**
> Just like the Emergency Stop, never recall your dog forward to pay for a position on the move. Walk all the way back to her side, click/feed in position, and then resume walking together.

---

### Step 3: Stand on the move.

The **Stand on the Move** is a classic advanced obedience skill because dogs naturally default to sitting when movement stops:

1. While walking slowly in **Heel**, glide a treat in front of her nose slightly forward (or flash a flat open palm backward toward her chest) as you say **Stand**, and smoothly take **1 step past her** while she stays planted on all four feet.
2. **Click** the instant she holds four feet still for 1 second, and return to feed at nose height (feeding high/forward keeps her standing rather than dropping into a sit!).
3. Fade the hand signal down to a crisp verbal **Stand**, and build to walking 5–10 steps ahead before turning back to reward.

---

### Step 4: Distance position box (Sit $\leftrightarrow$ Down $\leftrightarrow$ Stand at 15 feet).

Combine all three positions at a distance without letting your dog creep forward toward you:

1. Place a subtle marker line (a flat dowel or doorway threshold) right in front of your dog's front paws so you—and she—can clearly see if her front feet step forward.
2. Starting at 6 feet, cue transitions in varied pairs: **Sit $\rightarrow$ Down**, **Down $\rightarrow$ Stand**, **Stand $\rightarrow$ Sit**, **Down $\rightarrow$ Sit**.
3. Click and toss/deliver a treat right to her spot whenever her front paws stay pinned behind the line during the position change.
4. Gradually step back to 10 feet, then 15 feet.

---

### Step 5: Retrieve over a jump (Club Platinum classic).

Combine [Level 3 Jump](../../markdown/level-3/jump.md) and [Level 3 Retrieve](../../markdown/level-3/retrieve.md) using **back-chaining** and careful height safety:

> [!WARNING]
> **Jump Safety**
> Only use a jump height safe for your dog's age and structure (keep the bar low—elbow height or lower—with non-slip take-off and landing mats, and never jump young puppies over high bars).

1. **Practice the Return Jump First:** Leave your dog waiting on the _far_ side of a low jump holding her dumbbell/toy (or hand it to her there). Step back 6 feet on the landing side and cue **Come / Over!** Click as she clears the bar with the object still in her mouth and delivers it cleanly to your hand on **Thank You**.
2. **Add the Outbound Jump:** Set the dumbbell/toy 6 feet beyond the jump while you stand with your dog 5 feet in front of the jump. Ask for a calm **Wait** (do not let her break early!).
3. **Run the Full Sequence:** Say **Over / Get It** cleanly once. Wait quietly while she jumps out, picks up the object without dropping or mouthing it, jumps straight back over the bar, sits or stands squarely in front of you, and releases on **Thank You** for a huge jackpot!

#### Try It Cold

Run a miniature "Platinum Medley" in a fenced field or hall: **Off-Lead Heel** (30 seconds) $\rightarrow$ **Stand on the Move** $\rightarrow$ **Return to Side** $\rightarrow$ **30-second Watch** $\rightarrow$ **Retrieve Over a Low Jump**.
