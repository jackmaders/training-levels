# Level 1 Homework

"Wait just a dang minute here! If the DOG is going to school, how come the OWNER has to do homework?"

Sorry about that – don't look now, but it isn't really the DOG who's going to school. It's you!

My personal approach to homework is to read the question, stoutly declare that I know the answer, and then forget about it, so let's just be sure that you actually think about the answers a bit. Write them down, OK? Write them here in your training book, and you'll be able to go back from time to time and refresh your memory.

So here's your Level 1 homework:

> [!NOTE] Dogs' Letters to God
> Dear God,  
> We dogs can understand human verbal instructions,  
> hand signals,  
> whistles,  
> horns,  
> clickers,  
> beepers,  
> scent IDs,  
> electromagnetic energy fields,  
> and Frisbee flight paths.  
> What do humans understand?  
> 
> *— from a viral email called "Dogs' Letters to God"*
